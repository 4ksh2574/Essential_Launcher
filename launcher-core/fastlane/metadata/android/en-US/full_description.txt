<p><i>AX Home</i> is an Android home-screen app built on the Android launcher foundation, with Pixel-inspired features and customisation options.</p><p>AX Home includes a flexible home screen, app drawer, icon and layout options, wallpaper-aware colour theming, At a Glance widget support, and quick access to apps, contacts and web results.</p><p>Features and compatibility depend on the Android version and device.</p>
